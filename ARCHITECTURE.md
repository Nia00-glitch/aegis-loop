# Evidence-First Market & Product Intelligence OS

## Architecture Status
LOCKED BASELINE

## Core Principles

1. Evidence before conclusions.
2. No major recommendation without traceable evidence.
3. Loop Engineering controls research iteration.
4. Modules must remain replaceable.
5. Local-first and open/open-weight preferred.
6. Paid APIs are optional adapters, not core dependencies.
7. Models must remain replaceable.
8. No module may silently bypass verification.
9. Research, product analysis, and decisions must support critique and repair loops.
